Kraken parent Payward announced on October 14 that it had signed an agreement to acquire Saldo Finance Oy, the Finnish holding company of Lithuania-licensed Saldo Bank.

According to [Payward’s announcement](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-acquires-saldo-bank), completion is expected in 2027 and remains subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions. The acquisition has not been presented as a completed transaction.

## A banking layer for Europe

Payward says Saldo’s infrastructure would support its European plans for deposits, lending, cards, and services supplied to other financial-technology businesses.

Saldo already offers term deposits in Lithuania, Finland, Sweden, and Germany, according to the announcement, and has passporting into the Netherlands.

## What remains to happen

Approval, completion, and product rollout are separate milestones. An acquisition agreement does not mean every proposed service is immediately available to Kraken customers.

Compyvila’s reading: the development is significant because it concerns regulated banking infrastructure alongside crypto trading. Customers should still check which legal entity provides each service and which terms apply.
