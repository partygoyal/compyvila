## What it does

Unichain is an Ethereum Layer 2 developed by Uniswap Labs with a focus on decentralized finance. Its launch announcement describes an OP Stack network in the Superchain ecosystem, intended to support applications such as trading, lending, and liquidity provision. See the [original announcement](https://blog.uniswap.org/unichain-mainnet-is-here).

## The 2025 milestone

Public mainnet launched on **February 11, 2025**. This profile records that mainnet milestone, rather than the date Unichain was first announced or the founding date of Uniswap Labs. Earlier testnet activity belongs to a different stage of the project’s history.

## Network and token context

The [official network reference](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/unichain/technical-information/network-information) identifies ETH as the network currency. Paying gas with ETH is different from buying a token issued specifically for a new network. The token field is intentionally left blank here to avoid presenting an ecosystem or gas asset as a dedicated Unichain token.

## What to research

- Compare the current rollup, bridge, and withdrawal documentation before moving assets between networks.
- Identify the specific application and contract you intend to use; a network launch does not establish that every application is suitable for you.
- Separate liquidity incentives from sustained demand when evaluating ecosystem growth.
- Treat launch-day performance claims as dated statements, not measurements of today’s conditions.
