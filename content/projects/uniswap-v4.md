## What it does

Uniswap v4 is a release of the Uniswap decentralized exchange protocol. It introduces customizable hooks that can run logic around pool actions, alongside a shared pool-management architecture. The [protocol overview](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/overview) describes these building blocks.

## The 2025 milestone

The [official launch announcement](https://blog.uniswap.org/uniswap-v4-is-here) is dated **January 31, 2025** and records v4 becoming available across multiple networks. This listing represents a product launch. It does not imply that the wider Uniswap project was founded in 2025 or that earlier protocol versions first launched then.

## Network and token context

Uniswap v4 is deployed software rather than a standalone blockchain. Its deployments span multiple EVM networks. The token field is left blank because this listing does not describe the launch of a new v4 token. A protocol version, a pool’s traded assets, and a governance token are different concepts.

## What to research

- Identify the network, pool, and hook implementation involved in a specific interaction.
- Examine what a hook is permitted to do instead of assuming every v4 pool behaves identically.
- Distinguish protocol-level review from the review of a separate hook, frontend, or integration.
- Check price impact, liquidity, fees, and transaction permissions for the actual operation.

The launch announcement discusses security work completed before release. That historical statement should not be treated as a blanket assurance for every later extension or application. Research the exact components you intend to use.
