## A timestamped market observation

Those figures describe the report’s snapshot at 22:32 IST, rather than prices guaranteed to remain available. Crypto-related shares, including Strategy and Coinbase, were also lower in that update.

The simultaneous moves provide market context; they do not establish that one event explains every Bitcoin transaction.
