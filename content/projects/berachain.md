## What it does

Berachain is a Layer 1 network with an Ethereum-compatible execution environment. Its distinctive focus is Proof of Liquidity: an incentive design connecting the network with applications, validators, and liquidity. The [official introduction](https://docs.berachain.com/general/introduction/what-is-berachain) explains the architecture and the role of BeaconKit.

## The 2025 milestone

This listing records the mainnet launch on **February 6, 2025**. The [BERA disclosure whitepaper hosted by Kraken](https://assets-cms.kraken.com/files/51n36hrp/facade/0add1afb90e8ef5bd7bcde967601a8004db73a47.pdf), section D.8, documents that date. Earlier community activity and testnets do not change the year of this specific milestone.

## Network and token context

BERA is the network’s native gas and staking asset. Its role should be distinguished from other assets and reward mechanisms in the ecosystem. Check the [official BERA documentation](https://docs.berachain.com/general/tokens/bera) for the current description rather than assuming that every token using Berachain branding performs the same function.

## What to research

- Identify which assets, receipt tokens, and contracts a proposed activity involves.
- Separate protocol incentives from the economic performance of an individual application.
- Check withdrawal conditions, liquidity, and the consequences of changing reward rules.
- Review current documentation: a 2025 launch overview does not describe every later upgrade.

Our directory does not attach a safety rating or projected return to the network. An incentive mechanism explains how a system is intended to work; its name alone does not establish the outcome for an individual participant.
